# ISO 24495-1 checklist

[ISO 24495-1:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/78907.html) complements
ASD-STE100. It uses four interdependent reader outcomes for plain-language
information:

- **Relevant:** include the information the reader needs for the task.
- **Findable:** put the answer, action, limits, and key terms where the reader
  expects them.
- **Understandable:** show clear structure, terms, conditions, and relations.
- **Usable:** let the reader act, decide, or verify the result.

Use the outcomes together:

1. Name the reader, task, decision, or result.
2. Put the answer or next action early.
3. Match headings, lists, order, and examples to the task.
4. Remove information that does not help the reader complete the task.
5. Review the document with a representative task or reader when the result
   matters.

This is a practical review aid. It is not a copy of the ISO standard or proof
of formal conformance.
