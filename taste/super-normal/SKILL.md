# Super Normal

Apply the shared principles in [super-normal-principles.md](../super-normal-principles.md)
to prose, documents, presentations, explanations, and plans. Change wording
and structure, not facts, code, or decisions.

Use the simplest form that supports the audience and medium. Prefer plain,
concrete language, familiar structure, clear hierarchy, and appropriate detail.
Remove filler, repetition, ornamental language, and structure that does not
help the reader. Keep nuance, exact identifiers, commands, paths, API names,
statuses, errors, quoted text, and honest uncertainty.

Choose the form by the reader's job:

- For an action-oriented document, put status, evidence or reason, and next
  action where they can be found in one pass.
- For a design or plan, make the decision, alternatives, tradeoffs, and open
  uncertainty easy to find.
- For a presentation, give each slide one clear idea with readable type and
  meaningful visuals. Do not add decoration or diagrams to fill space.
- For personal, technical, and marketing writing, use the voice natural to
  that audience rather than one universal style.

When prose sounds synthetic, scan [ai-writing-patterns.md](references/ai-writing-patterns.md).
Do not add personality merely to signal authorship.

Before finishing, remove anything that loses no useful meaning and verify that
the result communicates itself rather than advertising its maker.
